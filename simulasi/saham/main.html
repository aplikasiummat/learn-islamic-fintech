            Trading unit and price steps

            1 lot = 100 shares. Prices move in IDX tick sizes (fraksi harga):

            Below Rp200Rp1
            Rp200 to below Rp500Rp2
            Rp500 to below Rp2.000Rp5
            Rp2.000 to below Rp5.000Rp10
            Rp5.000 and aboveRp25

            Auto rejection (ARA and ARB)

            Orders outside the daily band are rejected. Band from the previous close: Rp50 to Rp200, 35%; above Rp200 to Rp5.000, 25%; above Rp5.000, 20%. Simplified as symmetric up and down.

            Sessions

            • Pre-opening 08:45 to 09:00: orders queue, matched at the opening price
            • Session 1 09:00 to 12:00: continuous auction
            • Break 12:00 to 13:30: orders queue
            • Session 2 13:30 to 15:50: continuous auction
            • Pre-closing 15:50 to 16:00: orders queue, matched at the close

            Costs and settlement

            Buy fee 0.15%, sell fee 0.25% (includes the 0.1% final tax on sales). Sale money settles at T+2, so it is a receivable for two trading days. Dividends are paid to holders on the record day, and the price is adjusted on the next open.

            Sharia layer

            • SOTS: only DES stocks, no margin, no short selling
            • Akad: wakalah to your broker, bay' al-musawamah on the exchange, musyarakah as a shareholder
            • Primary market (IPO): prospectus, book building, OJK effective statement, e-IPO offering, pooling allotment, distribution, listing; the underwriter works under wakalah bil ujrah
            • Purify the non-halal share of dividends

            Macro and index events

            The economic calendar mirrors what moves the IDX: BPS inflation, the BI Rate decision (RDG BI), the US Fed, coal (HBA) and CPO prices, the rupiah, the MSCI index review and its rebalancing day, and the OJK review of the Daftar Efek Syariah each May and November. Prices react to the surprise against expectations, not to the news itself, and each sector reacts differently. Make your call before each decision.

            Simplifications

            Fictional stocks, simulated prices. One session is 10 trading days; each day lasts two minutes at 1×, so the session takes 20 minutes. Queued orders fill when the last price reaches your limit. No dividend tax, no margin calls. Rules follow IDX practice taught in class; check the latest IDX regulations before real trading.
