        What you are trading

        A sukuk certificate is a share in an asset, a usufruct, or a business, held for you by an SPV. Prices are quoted as a percentage of face value. Corporate names and SBSN series numbers here are fictional.

        Clean price, accrued ujrah, yield

        • The market quotes the clean price. When you buy, you also pay the seller's share of the next ujrah (the accrued part).
        • Price = present value of the remaining ujrah and the buy-back price, discounted at the market yield.
        • Yield up, price down. Long series move most.

        Which sukuk can trade?

        • Ijarah, mudharabah, wakalah (asset or equity) trade freely.
        • Murabahah is a receivable: selling it below face value is riba, so it cannot trade.
        • ST savings sukuk and CWLS are non-tradable by design; ST has an early redemption window.
        • SR may trade only after its holding period.

        Primary market

        • PBS auction: bid a yield. If your yield is at or below the cut-off you win, at your own yield (multiple price).
        • SR, ST, CWLS, corporate: order during the offer window, priced at par.

        Income and tax

        • SR, ST, CWLS pay monthly; corporates quarterly; PBS every six months.
        • Ujrah and profit share are taxed at a final 10%.
        • Mudharabah pays the holders' share (nisbah 70%) of actual profit. It can fall.
        • Broker fee on secondary trades: 0.10%.

        How your score is computed

        • 40 points: total return over 12 months.
        • 30 points: questions that pop up with market events.
        • 30 points: sharia conduct and investing behaviour (diversification, panic selling, churning, conventional bonds).

        Simplifications

        Settlement is instant (in reality T+2), the yield curve is stylised, ratings and events are scripted with random outcomes, and coupon dates are rounded to month ends.
